Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Lemango builds simple, browser-based tools. This page explains what information is and isn't handled when you use lemango.org and our products: Lemango AI, Court & Notice Management ("Case Manager"), and FastPay Studio.

The short version: our tools run in your browser and don't require an account. We don't sell your data. We only receive information that you choose to send us (for example, an email asking to subscribe to updates).

Information you give us

  • Newsletter / contact: if you email us to subscribe, we receive your email address and anything you include in the message.
  • Tool inputs: content you type into Lemango AI, Case Manager, or FastPay Studio is processed in your browser. Documents you create (such as FastPay PDFs) are generated on your device and are not uploaded to us.

    • Information collected automatically

    Like most websites, our hosting and content-delivery providers may process standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited in order to serve the site securely and prevent abuse. We do not use this to identify you personally.

    Cookies, analytics, and advertising

    • We may use privacy-focused, aggregate analytics to understand general traffic.
    • We may display third-party advertising (for example, Google AdSense). Ad providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and may personalize ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can control personalized advertising through your Google Ads settings.
    • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

    Third-party services

    Depending on the page, we load services such as GitHub Pages (hosting), Cloudflare (CDN/security), Google Fonts, Google AdSense, and (for exported documents or sign-in) Google. Each has its own privacy policy.

    Data retention

    We keep subscription emails only as long as needed to send you updates, and you can ask us to remove you at any time. Tool data you create stays in your browser and is deleted when you clear your browser storage.

    Children

    Our tools are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

    Your choices

    • Unsubscribe from emails by replying "unsubscribe" to any message.
    • Disable cookies or third-party scripts in your browser.
    • Contact us to request deletion of any information you've sent us.

    Changes

    We may update this policy as the products evolve. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

    Contact

    Questions? Email lemangonewsletter@gmail.com.
