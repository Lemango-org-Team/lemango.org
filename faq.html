Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Is Lemango free?

Yes. All current tools are free to use. Paid tiers may arrive later and will be announced on the Payments page.

Do I need an account?

No. Lemango AI, Case Manager, and FastPay Studio all work without an account. Sign-in is optional and currently in preview.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser. We do not run a large backend database of user content. See the Privacy Policy and Security pages.

Does Lemango AI store my conversations?

Conversations are kept in your browser's local storage so you can revisit them. They are not uploaded to us.

Are payments available?

No. Payments and subscriptions are not available yet. See the Payments page.

Is Case Manager legal advice?

No. It is independent organizational software for managing cases, documents, and notices. It is not legal advice or an official court service.

How do I report a bug or security issue?

Email lemangonewsletter@gmail.com. For security issues, please report responsibly (see the Security page).

How do I subscribe to updates?

Use the newsletter form on the home page, which opens a pre-filled email. We'll add you to the list.
