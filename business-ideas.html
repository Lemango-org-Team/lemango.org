12 Website Ideas People Would Pay For

Profitable micro-SaaS ideas with clear customers, pricing, and competitive advantages

1

AI Business Automation Platform

High Potential

Description

A website that automates repetitive work for small businesses: replying to customers, creating quotes, organizing leads, and drafting emails.

Customers

Small businesses, freelancers, agencies

Why They Pay

It saves time and helps them serve more customers. AI adoption among small businesses is growing.

Standout Feature

Pre-built workflows for common business tasks

Potential Pricing: $19–$99/month
2

Appointment Booking Website

Description

Let barbers, tutors, mechanics, and cleaners accept bookings, send reminders, and manage cancellations.

Customers

Local service businesses

Standout Feature

Automatic reminders and a booking page for each business

Potential Pricing: $15–$49/month
3

Invoice and Payment Management

Description

Generate invoices, track unpaid bills, calculate expenses, and send payment reminders.

Customers

Freelancers, contractors, small businesses

Why They Pay

Billing and collecting payments are established business software needs.

Potential Pricing: $9–$39/month
4

Social Media Content Manager

Description

Generate post ideas, create captions, organize content calendars, and track engagement across platforms.

Customers

Creators, influencers, local businesses

Standout Feature

Turn one business update into a week of platform-specific posts

Potential Pricing: $10–$39/month
5

Website Monitoring and Security Alerts

Description

Check whether websites are online, detect broken pages, monitor SSL certificate expiration, and alert owners to suspicious changes.

Customers

Website owners, developers, agencies

Standout Feature

A single dashboard for monitoring multiple websites

Potential Pricing: $5–$49/month
6

Resume and Job Application Assistant

Description

Help users improve resumes, tailor applications to job descriptions, and track applications.

Customers

Job seekers, college students, career changers

Standout Feature

One-click resume tailoring for a specific job

Potential Pricing: $5–$25/month or a one-time fee
7

Interactive Learning Platform

Description

Teach programming, math, science, or other skills with interactive lessons, practice problems, and progress tracking.

Customers

Students, parents, teachers

Standout Feature

Lessons that adapt to what a learner gets wrong

Potential Pricing: $5–$20/month
8

Price Tracking and Deal Alerts

Description

Track product prices and notify users when prices fall below their target.

Customers

Online shoppers, deal hunters, collectors

Standout Feature

Price history charts and customizable alerts

Potential Pricing: $3–$10/month, plus possible affiliate revenue
9

Simple CRM for a Specific Industry

Description

Track customers, follow-ups, estimates, and sales in one place. Focus on one niche, such as cleaning companies or independent tutors.

Customers

Small service businesses

Standout Feature

A specialized workflow instead of a complicated generic CRM

Potential Pricing: $19–$79/month
10

Freelancer Client Portal

Description

Let freelancers share files, track project progress, approve deliverables, and manage client communication.

Customers

Designers, developers, video editors

Standout Feature

A branded portal for each client

Potential Pricing: $9–$29/month
11

Document Analysis Tool

Description

Let users upload long PDFs and extract summaries, key information, tables, and answers to questions.

Customers

Researchers, professionals, students

Standout Feature

Search across multiple documents with page references

Important Note

You'll need to manage AI processing costs and protect uploaded documents.

Potential Pricing: $5–$25/month
12

Niche Business Directory

Description

Create a directory for one category, such as tutors, wedding photographers, home repair services, or local gaming coaches.

Customers

Businesses paying for qualified leads or enhanced listings

Standout Feature

Verified profiles and real customer inquiries

Potential Pricing: $20–$200/month for premium listings
