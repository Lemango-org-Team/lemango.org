Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy describes information handled when you use lemango.org and Lemango AI, Court & Notice Management ("Case Manager"), FastPay Studio, and related services. Some features use browser storage; sign-in, AI requests, newsletter signups, and Case Manager records also involve server-side processing.

In brief: We do not sell personal information. Google sign-in provides basic profile information, AI requests are sent to AI providers, and Case Manager records and some service logs are stored on our backend. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or information you are not comfortable sharing with the relevant service providers.

Information we process

  • Google sign-in: Google provides an account identifier, email address, name, and profile image when available. We use this information to create an account record and authenticate your session. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Lemango AI: Prompts, conversation context, and model choices are sent to our backend and then to an external AI provider to generate responses. Service logs may include conversation text, timestamps, model and session identifiers, IP address, and, for signed-in requests, your email address or a short message excerpt.
  • Case Manager: Case details, notices, document metadata, and session records are sent to and stored on our backend, associated with the signed-in account that created them. These records are not limited to your browser.
  • Newsletter: A signup can include your email address, optional name, signup source, timestamp, and IP address. This information is stored on the backend and used to manage the newsletter and send signup-related email.
  • Browser-based tools: Lemango AI chat history and some preferences, workspace files, notes, or media are stored in your browser using local storage or IndexedDB. Clearing browser storage removes those local copies, but does not delete information already sent to our backend or a third party.
  • Forge bring-your-own-key settings: if you enter your own AI provider key, Forge stores it in this browser and sends it directly to the provider you select. This feature is designed not to send that key to the Lemango backend. Use only a key you control and avoid saving it on a shared device.
  • Technical data: Our hosting, CDN, analytics, and advertising providers may process IP address, browser/device details, request times, and pages or resources requested.

How information is used

We use information to provide and maintain features, authenticate accounts, store Case Manager records, generate AI responses, manage newsletter requests, enforce usage limits, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and understand site traffic. We may also use or disclose information when reasonably necessary to protect users or the service, comply with law, or respond to a valid legal request.

Third-party services

Depending on the feature and selected model, information may be processed by Google (sign-in, analytics, advertising, and related services), Cloudflare (CDN and security), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Render (backend hosting), and AI providers such as Pollinations, Groq, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Hugging Face, DeepSeek, OpenAI, or Anthropic. Some browser-based AI features may send content and, when you choose Forge's bring-your-own-key option, your provider key directly to the provider you select. These providers have their own privacy practices and may process information under their own terms. We do not control their retention or use of submitted content.

Cookies and browser storage

Signing in uses an essential session cookie to keep your account authenticated. The cookie is configured as HttpOnly and uses secure transport settings in production. The site also uses local storage or IndexedDB for some product data and preferences. Google Analytics and Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies and browser storage through your browser; blocking them may affect features. Personalized advertising can be managed through Google Ads settings.

Retention and deletion requests

Account records, Case Manager data, newsletter signups, and operational or security logs are stored on the backend. We do not currently publish a fixed retention period for all of these records, and some logs or backups may remain for a time after a request. You can email us to request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your account or email address. We will review the request and explain any limits that apply.

Security and incident updates

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, but no online service can promise absolute security. Our current service status and incident updates are published on the Status page; our security practices and reporting contact are on the Security page.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the services change. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, email lemangonewsletter@gmail.com.
