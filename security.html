Security

Security at Lemango

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Lemango is built to keep your data under your control. Most of our tools run entirely in your browser, and we do not operate a large backend that stores your personal information.

The short version: your documents, chats, and case data stay on your device. We do not sell or share your data. If you find a security issue, please report it responsibly.

How your data is handled

  • Lemango AI: messages are sent to our AI provider to generate a reply. We do not store your conversation history on a server; it lives in your browser's local storage.
  • Case Manager: cases and documents are kept in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to export or share a file.
  • FastPay Studio: agreements and signatures are generated on your device. PDFs are created locally and downloaded directly to you.

Sign-in and accounts

When account sign-in is available, we use Google's OAuth 2.0 flow. We never see or store your Google password. Session cookies are httpOnly and signed. We ask only for basic profile information (name, email, avatar).

Payments

Payments are not available yet. Lemango does not currently process payments or subscriptions. Any payment-related UI you see is a demonstration only — no card is charged and no data is collected. We will clearly announce when real payments become available.

How we protect the site

  • HTTPS everywhere, via our hosting and CDN providers.
  • Security headers and content security practices applied at the edge.
  • No third-party tracking scripts beyond what is needed for analytics and advertising, which you can control.
  • Regular dependency and configuration reviews.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly by emailing lemangonewsletter@gmail.com with a description and steps to reproduce. Please do not publicly disclose the issue until we have had a reasonable opportunity to address it. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will acknowledge your report.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run a large backend database of user content.
  • We do not process payments or store payment details.

Contact

Security questions? Email lemangonewsletter@gmail.com.
